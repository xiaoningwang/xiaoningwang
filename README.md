# Hi, I'm Xiaoning Wang 👋

**Associate Professor · School of Data Science and Media Intelligence · Communication University of China**

I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Renmin University of China in 2019, supervised by Prof. Yongjin Jin. My work connects statistical methodology with media applications and practical learning tools.

[Website](https://xiaoningwang.github.io/) · [Publications](https://xiaoningwang.github.io/publications/) · [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-4060-5982) · [Email](mailto:sdwangxiaoning@foxmail.com)

## Research interests

- **Survey methodology & data integration** — complex sampling, split questionnaire designs, sample integration, missing data, and data quality.
- **Media data analysis & machine learning** — text mining, sentiment analysis, social networks, information diffusion, and deep learning.
- **Large language models & agents** — prompt engineering, multi-agent systems, and applications in education and research.
