## About me

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Data Science and Media Intelligence at the Communication University of China. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Renmin University of China in 2019, supervised by Prof. Yongjin Jin.

My work brings together survey methodology, statistical learning, and media applications. In the classroom, I emphasize hands-on projects: collecting real data, asking clear questions, building models, and communicating evidence.

Our team is developing **[Scholar Hero](https://scholarhero.cn/)**, an intelligent teaching assistant powered by large language models and agents, to support classroom demonstrations, coding guidance, and personalized feedback.

## Research interests

- **Survey methodology & data integration** — complex sampling, split questionnaire designs, sample integration, missing data, and data quality.
- **Media data analysis** — text mining, sentiment analysis, social networks, and information diffusion.
- **Machine learning** — statistical learning and deep learning for media and social data.
- **Large language models & agents** — prompt engineering, multi-agent systems, and applications in teaching and research.
